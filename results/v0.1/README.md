# UAS v0.1 held-out-task results

This directory contains the first complete UAS governed-memory benchmark. It compares learning on the held-out continuous-control task under four conditions: no memory, naive retrieval, similarity retrieval, and governed retrieval.

## Result

All four conditions reached a final mean success rate of `1.0` across three seeds. Memory did not produce a clear general advantage in this small experiment:

| Condition | Retrieved episodes | Successful episodes | Prefilled transitions | First 0.8 success, mean steps | Sustained 0.8 success, mean steps | Normalized success AUC |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No memory | 0 | 0 | 0 | 750 | 1,500 | 0.825 |
| Naive | 48 | 24 | 4,062 | 667 | 1,333 | 0.855 |
| Similarity | 48 | 24 | 4,062 | 500 | 2,417 | 0.813 |
| Governed | 24 | 24 | 462 | 833 | 1,917 | 0.790 |

Naive retrieval had the highest overall area under the learning curve and reached sustained success slightly earlier than the no-memory baseline. Similarity retrieval crossed the success threshold earliest, but later regressed and was less stable. Governed retrieval did not outperform the baseline in threshold timing, sustained success, or area under the curve.

These results show that the end-to-end memory, retrieval, replay-prefill, training, and evaluation pipeline works. They do **not** establish that governed memory improves transfer. In particular, the conditions did not receive equal numbers of replay transitions: naive and similarity retrieval supplied 4,062 transitions, while governed retrieval supplied 462. The experiment therefore mixes retrieval quality with replay volume.

“First 0.8 success” is the first evaluation at or above the configured `0.8` threshold, averaged over seeds. “Sustained 0.8 success” is the earliest evaluation after which that seed stayed at or above `0.8` for every remaining evaluation. Normalized success AUC is the trapezoidal area under the mean success-rate curve divided by 5,000 training steps. The latter two measures are derived from `learning_curves.csv`; the configured summary reports the first-threshold measure.

## Configuration

- Held-out task: `held_out`
- Conditions: `no_memory`, `naive`, `similarity`, `governed`
- Seeds: 0, 1, and 2
- Training: 5,000 environment steps per condition and seed
- Evaluation: every 250 steps, using 10 episodes
- Source memory: 48 episodes from `open`, `upper_gap`, and `lower_gap`
- Total evaluations: 252 rows, covering all 12 condition/seed runs

This is an early prototype result with only three seeds, a small evaluation sample, and tasks from one simple environment family. It has no robot or cross-simulator evidence, and the differences shown here should not be treated as statistically conclusive. A stronger follow-up should equalize replay-transition counts, use more seeds, add uncertainty estimates, and test harder held-out tasks.
